About me

Rooted in movement,
built on breath.

Hi! My name is Michaela. Movement has always been a prominent part of my life. I grew up in Winston-Salem, NC, and started dancing when I was 3 years old. Dance shaped who I am today, strengthening my work ethic and fostering my creative identity. More importantly, dance empowered me to share movement with others. My first job was in the studio, demonstrating tap combos and tying little ballet shoes.

At 16, I was introduced to hot yoga in the style of 26 postures -- I was hooked. I drove myself to the YMCA to join class at 6am before school every Friday. Fast-forward through 10 years of ebbing, flowing, and always coming back to the mat, I finally earned my RYT 200 certification! Now, I'm seeking to build community in Raleigh, NC and deepen my own relationship with the 8-limb path. I'm most comfortable teaching vinyasa and love to help others find their own strength, curiosity, and freedom on the mat!
